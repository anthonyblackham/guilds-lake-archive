Guild's Lake Archive Maps & data

Historic topography

The ground before it was cut and filled.

Contours and spot heights traced from the historic surveys of Guild's Lake and the hills behind it, from 1896 to about 1910, before and during the sluicing and filling that reshaped the ground. Every line carries the height printed on the sheet and its height on the City of Portland datum, how it was traced, and a link to the sheet it came from.
